Place your PDF exam file here.

Rename it to  exam.pdf  (or update pdfFile in config.js to match your filename).

Example:
  - File you copy here:  midterm2025.pdf
  - Setting in config.js:  pdfFile: "pdf/midterm2025.pdf"

IMPORTANT:
  Do NOT rename or delete this folder.
  The viewer will look for the PDF inside  pdf/
