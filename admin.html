🛡️ 4. Security & Restriction Features
Turn individual protections ON or OFF depending on your practical exam needs.
🚫 Block Right-Click Context Menu
Prevents right-clicking on pages (disables "Save Image", "Inspect", etc.)
📋 Block Copy, Cut & Drag
Prevents students from copying text or dragging elements out of the page.
✏️ Block Text Highlighting & Selection
Disables mouse cursor selection and highlighting on the entire exam view.
⌨️ Block Keyboard Shortcuts
Disables Print (Ctrl+P), Save (Ctrl+S), DevTools (F12, Ctrl+Shift+I), View Source (Ctrl+U).
⚠️ Tab-Switch Blackout Alert
Blacks out the screen and displays a warning if a student switches tabs or minimizes window.
👁️ Anti-Screenshot: Blur When Mouse Leaves Window
Instantly blurs and shields the exam when the mouse moves outside the window (blocks Snipping Tool clicks and dual-monitor cheating).
💧 Diagonal Watermark on Pages
Stamps a subtle diagonal watermark across all rendered PDF pages.
Text printed diagonally across pages when watermark is enabled.
